2Run this in a terminal on this laptop

Needs Node.js 20 or newer. It downloads a small agent, serves your data to this laptop only, and starts a DCP worker.

3Watch the coordinator screen

Your laptop appears in the diagram as soon as the agent connects. Leave the terminal open while training runs; press Ctrl+C to leave.

  • The agent never sends your rows. It sends their count and feature names, and during training only gradients, Hessians and losses.
  • Your data is served on localhost only, so no other machine can read it.
